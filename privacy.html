PRIVACY POLICY

Effective October 21, 2026

Night City Breach is a free browser game made by an independent developer in Arizona, USA. There are no accounts, no ads, and no in-app purchases. This page explains what little data the game handles.

This is an unofficial fan work and is not approved/endorsed by CD PROJEKT RED. Cyberpunk, Cyberpunk 2077 and related names belong to their respective owners.

Stored on your device

Your progress (unlocked districts, stars, best scores, difficulty) and settings (sound, music, and your analytics choice) are saved in your browser's local storage. Your current session's score is kept in session storage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Use WIPE SAVE in the menu, or clear this site's data in your browser, to delete it.

The game also caches its own files on your device so it can run offline.

Analytics (only if you accept)

If you tap ACCEPT on the analytics prompt, the game loads Google Analytics to help us understand how the game is played. If you decline or never answer, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

When analytics is on, Google Analytics collects:

  • Pages viewed and gameplay events: district started, wave started and district finished (with difficulty, wave reached, stars, lives left and score), and in-game actions such as towers built, upgraded and sold, quickhacks cast, towers purged, and daemons uploaded in a breach.
  • Device and browser information such as device type, operating system, screen size and language.
  • An approximate location (country, region or city) that Google derives from your IP address. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.
  • A random identifier stored in the _ga cookies so repeat visits can be counted.

We use this only as aggregate statistics. Advertising features and ad personalization are turned off. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not combine it with any other information. Google processes this data under its own terms; see how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in our Google Analytics account (2 months by default) and then deleted by Google.

Changing your choice

Use the ANALYTICS: ON/OFF button on the main menu at any time. Turning it off stops analytics immediately and deletes the _ga cookies. You can also block analytics with your browser's settings or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. Because analytics only runs after you opt in, the game does not respond differently to "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signals.

Hosting

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when your browser downloads the game, for security and operations, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not have access to those logs.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Analytics stays off unless someone on the device opts in.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
