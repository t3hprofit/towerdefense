HOW TO PLAY

BUILD

Tap a tower in the BUILD tray, then tap an empty cell to place it. Tap a placed tower to upgrade, sell, or purge it. Enemies that reach the end damage your CORE.

QUICKHACKS

Quickhacks cost RAM. Select one, then tap an enemy. RAM regenerates over time. ICE Decks regenerate it faster. Some enemies and every boss carry an ICE shield: towers do a fraction of their damage until any quickhack breaks it. Arc Nodes punch through shields better than other towers. On Legend and Blackwall, breaking a shield refunds some RAM.

NETRUNNERS

Enemy netrunners hack your towers and take them OFFLINE. An ICE Deck's firewall protects towers in its range. Tap an offline tower and PURGE it to bring it back fast.

BREACH PROTOCOL

Between waves, breach the local net. Pick codes from the matrix, alternating rows and columns, to upload daemons: cheaper towers, extra eddies, vulnerable enemies, and more. You start with one breach charge and earn another every three waves. The wave clock keeps running while you breach (you get +10s), so be quick.

WAVE CLOCK

The next wave starts on its own when the clock runs out. Call it early for bonus score.

ACCESS POINTS

Glowing nodes on the map can be jacked into for RAM: hijack turrets, trigger traps, slam blast doors, or reroute the enemy path.
